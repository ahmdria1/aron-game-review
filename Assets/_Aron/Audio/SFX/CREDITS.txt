Weather and breakable sound effects (Audio/SFX/Resources/AronSfx)
=======================================================

All files were edited for the game: trimmed, layered, loop-crossfaded,
loudness-normalized and encoded as Ogg Vorbis.

Original sounds
---------------
All other sounds in this folder (foliage and palm loops, gusts, creaks, wind, waves, sand, sandstorm, wall crumbles and boss hits)
are original sounds made for this project, as are the sound layers added to crate_break_1, explosion_1, explosion_2 and thunder_near_1.
Proof of origin is kept by the owner outside this repository.
