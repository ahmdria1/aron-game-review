# Weather and sky

Dynamic, data-driven weather and a real sky for every stage. The rules are plain C# in `Aron.Logic` (tested outside
Unity); the Unity side lives in `Assets/_Aron/Scripts/Atmosphere` and five shaders in `Assets/_Aron/Resources/Shaders`.

## Data

`Assets/_Aron/Resources/Weather/weather.json` is generated by `python3 tools/weather_table.py`. Do not hand-edit it;
change the script and run it again.

- 34 countries, each with a climate type, hemisphere, place (lat/lon), UTC offset, base wind, sea and river water, and
  weights for the eight weathers (clear, clouds, haze, rain, thunderstorm, fog, sandstorm, snow) per season.
- Month overrides where a season is too coarse: Japan's tsuyu (June, July) and September typhoon rain; the Indian
  (June–September) and Pakistani (July–August) monsoon; Oman's khareef (July–August); Malaysia's north-east monsoon
  (November–January) and the August–September haze.
- Region overrides for Saudi Arabia: Asir and Al-Bahah are foggy and never get sandstorms (Asir rivers are mountain
  streams); Jazan has no sand; Tabuk (Jabal al-Lawz) gets winter snow; the Eastern Province uses Gulf water and
  Makkah (Jeddah) the Red Sea.
- The southern hemisphere (Brazil, South Africa, Indonesia) has its seasons flipped.
- 15 water profiles: red_sea, gulf, arabian_sea, mediterranean, atlantic, atlantic_tropical, atlantic_cold, north_sea,
  pacific, tropical_sea, nile, amazon, iraq_marshes, river, mountain_river.
- The numbers are a simplification of published climate normals (WMO 1991–2020 as published by national met
  services) for play. They are **not a forecast** and do not claim to match any day's real weather.

## How a weather is chosen

`WeatherSchedule.At(table, country, region, utc)` splits the local day into slots of 8–20 minutes, each seeded from the
region and slot number, so every player in the same region sees the same weather at the same time. The last
`blendSeconds` (30 s) of each slot blends smoothly into the next.

`WeatherPolicy.Resolve(mode, settings, authored, trackWeather, dynamic)` then applies the play rules:

| Mode | Weather |
|---|---|
| Race, time trial | The track's fixed weather from `tracks.json` (clear, clouds, haze, or snow on ski tracks only). Leaderboards stay fair. |
| Boss | The stage's authored weather, fixed. |
| Story | Authored non-clear weather stays fixed. Otherwise the dynamic weather, made gentle: thunderstorms become rain and sandstorms become haze. |
| Free play (a finished stage) | The full dynamic weather. |
| Toggle off ("Live weather") | The authored weather, fixed, everywhere. |

Weather is cosmetic by default. The only gameplay effect is optional (the "Weather effects in free play" toggle,
off by default): in free play only, wet or snowy ground lowers grip a little (`1 − 0.15·wetness − 0.2·snow`).
Authored rain stages keep their existing slippery track.

## Sky

- Sun and moon positions come from low-precision Meeus formulas for the stage's place, for **today's real date**.
  The stage's authored time of day (day, sunset or night) picks the hour; sunset is half an hour before the sun
  really sets there that day.
- The moon's phase, its lit fraction and the angle of its bright edge are real, so the Ramadan and Eid crescents are
  the right way round. Only the compass bearing of the whole sky is turned so the sun or moon sits where the camera can
  see it; heights, phase and limb angle are not changed.
- Six parts of the day (Fajr, morning, noon, Asr, Maghrib, night) follow from the sun's height and direction, with
  colour keys for the blue hour and the orange band at Fajr and Maghrib.
- Stars come out below −6° and the Milky Way on clear, moonless nights; both fade behind cloud, haze, fog and a bright
  moon. The star field turns with local sidereal time.

## Rendering by tier (`WeatherQuality.For`)

| | Low | Medium | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rain drops / snow flakes | 600 / 400 | 1600 / 900 | 3600 / 2000 |
| Rain splashes | none | 16 | 40 |
| Clouds | painted layer in the sky dome | painted layer | painted layer + lit cloud billboards |
| Wet ground | darker | darker + glossy, puddles with ripples | same |
| Water | scrolled normals, foam, depth colour, sun glint | + probe reflection | + vertex waves; planar reflection if the toggle is on |
| Lightning | sky flash | + flash light | + flash light |
| Update rate | 5 Hz | 10 Hz | 20 Hz |

Shaders (in `Resources/Shaders` so `Shader.Find` works in builds; keywords use `multi_compile` so no variant is
stripped): `Aron/Sky`, `Aron/Water` (`_ARON_WAVES`, `_ARON_PLANAR`), `Aron/Rain` (`_ARON_SNOW`), `Aron/Cloud`,
`Aron/Puddle`. If a shader is missing, the stage falls back to the old look (solid-colour sky, particle rain, flat
water).

## Lightning and accessibility

`Lightning.Plan` spaces strikes at least 4 s apart. Thunder is delayed by distance at 343 m/s; strikes under 1.5 km use
the near thunder clips. With **Reduce flashing** on (main menu), a strike is a slow, dim
swell (0.5 s up, 1 s down, 12 % extra light) instead of sharp flickers. Normal flickers last 0.08 s, start 0.35 s
apart, and never exceed three in a second.

## Audio

`WeatherAudio` keeps its rain and sand layers and gains two beds: `wind_loop` and `waves_loop` (near water). Gains come
from `AmbienceBeds.For(look, nearWater, underCover)`; under cover the beds duck. Thunder uses the existing
`thunder_near_*` and `thunder_far_*` clips. Both loops are final sounds.

## Settings (main menu)

- **Live weather** (`weather.dynamicWeather`, default on).
- **Weather effects in free play** (`weather.freePlayEffects`, default off).
- **Reduce flashing** (`access.reduceFlash`, default off).
- `weather.planarReflections` (default off) exists in the save but has no menu button yet; it only takes effect on
  high-tier phones.

## Tests

`Tests/Aron.Logic.Tests/WeatherSkyTests.cs` covers: valid tables for all 34 countries and every month; Gulf summer is
mostly clear and hazy with no rain; Abha fog and no sand; Japan's June rain and the Indian monsoon; flipped southern

the Ramadan 1446 crescent over Riyadh; the lit edge facing the sun; the sun following the clock; the six day parts in

## Trees and palms in the wind

Yes: palms and trees move when it is windy, and they rustle.

- **Shader** `Resources/Shaders/AronFoliageWind.shader` (+ `AronFoliageWind.hlsl`), URP forward. Vertex sway driven by two globals the
  weather sets every frame: `_AronFoliageWind` (direction, tip sway in metres, speed in Hz) and `_AronFoliageFlutter` (frond flutter, flutter
  speed, gust 0..1, gust share). The sway weight goes trunk to tip: vertex colour red if the mesh has it (the stage builders bake it),
  else UV2 x, else the vertex height. Frond flutter comes from vertex colour green / UV2 y. The phase comes from each object's
  position, so no two trees are in step, and the wind is a wave that travels downwind so neighbours lean one after the other.
  Gust pulses add to the lean. The Low tier compiles `ARON_FOLIAGE_LOW`: one sine, no flutter, no gust. All material data is in
  `UnityPerMaterial` (SRP Batcher) and `multi_compile_instancing` is on. The shadow and depth passes sway too.
- **Runtime** `Atmosphere/FoliageWind.cs`: the weather director gives it wind, dust and rain at its tick rate, and it eases to them and sets the
  globals each frame. The maths is `Logic/Weather/FoliageWind.cs` (`FoliageWindMath`), numbers in `Resources/Config/foliage_wind.json`:
  idle sway 2 cm up to 30 cm at full wind, x1.3 at full dust, x1.12 at full rain, ceiling 40 cm. **Reduce motion** scales sway, flutter and gusts
  by `reduceMotionWindShare` (0.4) from `weather_gameplay.json`, the same share as the wind push on the hero.
- **Where it is used:** `StageArt.Foliage`, `FoliageCyl`, `FoliageFrustum` make sway meshes (one material per colour, shared); the landmark
  factory uses them for palms, trees (cedar, dragon tree, rainforest, black forest, cherry, lone tree), acacia, gum forest and bamboo.
  The old per-frond CPU sway (`WindSway`) is now only for sails and flags. Without the shader in the build, the builders fall back to plain colours.
- **Sound** `Audio/FoliageAudio.cs`: three palm loops (`palm_rustle_light`, `_moderate`, `_strong`) and three leaves loops (`leaves_rustle_light`, `_medium`, `_strong`),
  each a 10-20 s seamless loop. `FoliageWindFile.LayerMix` crossfades them with equal power as the wind rises (mid layer from `layerMidAt`, strong from `layerStrongAt`,
  blend width `layerBlend`); on the low tier only one layer plays at a time. Volume and pitch follow the wind and the distance to the nearest palms or trees
  (full inside 3 m, silent beyond 24 m). One-shots: `frond_creak_1..3` in gusts close to foliage (at most one every 3.5-9 s), and `foliage_gust_1..4` whooshes
  for a real gust near foliage (`gustSound*` keys in `foliage_wind.json`). Reduce motion scales the gust, so the whooshes follow it. Credits are in `Audio/SFX/CREDITS.txt`.

## Not verified

There is no Unity licence on the build machine, so the Atmosphere scripts and the shaders have only been
syntax-checked. They have not been compiled by Unity, run on a device, or profiled. Check on device: shader compile
on Android (GLES3/Vulkan) and iOS (Metal), frame time on a low-tier phone in rain, and the planar reflection on a
high-tier phone. The foliage shader (all three passes, the keyword switch, SRP Batcher compatibility in the Frame Debugger, the baked
vertex colours on the primitive meshes) and the rustle levels are also unchecked in Unity.

Weather that changes how the game plays (wind push, dust visibility, traction, heat) is described in `docs/feel_and_systems.md`.
